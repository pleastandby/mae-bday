# Project: A Little Birthday Corner for Mae

## 1. Concept

Create a beautiful, minimal, interactive birthday website for Mae.

The concept is:

**"A Little Corner of the Internet, Just to Celebrate You."**

This is a platonic birthday gift from someone who genuinely appreciates her.

The website should communicate warmth, admiration, gratitude, and good wishes without feeling romantic, intimate, or emotionally demanding.

It should feel like a thoughtful digital gesture from a friend.

## 2. Visual Direction

Reuse the elegant, dreamy, slightly whimsical aesthetic of the previous birthday website.

Visual characteristics:
- Warm, soft backgrounds.
- Subtle pastel gradients.
- Delicate floating particles and stars.
- Editorial typography.
- Minimal illustrations.
- Gentle transitions.
- A little nostalgic, scrapbook-like charm.
- Plenty of whitespace.

Use a restrained palette of warm ivory, muted lavender, dusty pink, and soft gold.

Typography:
- Elegant serif for headings.
- DM Sans or Inter for body text.
- Occasional handwritten annotations for personality.

Avoid excessive decorations, overly pink romantic visuals, heart animations, and anything resembling a Valentine's Day website.

## 3. Website Experience

### Scene 01 — A Little Invitation

A minimal opening screen.

Text:

"Hey, Mae."

Below it:

"There's a little something waiting for you."

Include a small illustrated envelope or gift and a button:

"Open your surprise"

On clicking, smoothly reveal the main experience.

### Scene 02 — The Birthday Greeting

A beautiful editorial hero section.

Main heading:

"Today is a good day to celebrate someone wonderful."

Highlight:

"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MAE!"

Supporting text:

"Another year, another collection of memories, discoveries, unexpected adventures, and little reasons to smile."

Include subtle floating stars and gentle motion.

### Scene 03 — Little Things Worth Celebrating

Create a collection of three or four interactive cards.

Suggested headings:

- "Your curious little universe"
- "The things that make you unique"
- "A little appreciation"
- "Here's to new adventures"

Each card should reveal a short, editable message when clicked.

Keep the messages genuine and friendship-oriented. Don't invent personal memories or specific personality traits.

### Scene 04 — A Little Gallery

Create a simple photo gallery using replaceable image placeholders.

Design:
- Polaroid-inspired frames.
- Subtle rotations.
- Small captions.
- Soft shadows.
- Click-to-expand lightbox.

Keep the gallery optional and make it easy to remove if there aren't enough suitable photographs.

### Scene 05 — A Birthday Note

A minimal letter-style section.

Heading:

"A little note for you."

Message:

"Hey Mae,

Happy birthday!

I hope this new chapter brings you beautiful experiences, exciting discoveries, unexpected opportunities, and plenty of reasons to smile.

Keep exploring the things that interest you, keep being curious, and never stop working towards the things that matter to you.

I'm genuinely glad our paths crossed, and I hope life continues to surprise you in wonderful ways.

Wishing you happiness, good health, meaningful experiences, and a year worth remembering.

Have a wonderful birthday, Mae!"

Keep this message editable.

The tone should be sincere and appreciative, not romantic or sentimental to the point of emotional pressure.

### Scene 06 — One Last Little Surprise

Create a small interactive gift box.

When clicked:
- The box opens.
- A few stars or confetti particles appear.
- A hidden message is revealed.

Message:

"Here's to another year of being wonderfully you."

Add a small playful line:

"Achievement unlocked: Another trip around the sun!"

### Scene 07 — The Final Screen

A clean birthday celebration.

Large heading:

"HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MAE!"

Supporting text:

"May this year be kind to you, full of discoveries, and filled with moments worth remembering."

Finish with:

"Made with care, just for your birthday."

Include a subtle replay button.

## 4. Animation and Interaction

Use Framer Motion for:
- Smooth scene transitions.
- Gentle fade-ins.
- Staggered text reveals.
- Floating decorative elements.
- Interactive cards.
- Gift-opening animation.
- Lightbox transitions.

Keep everything subtle and polished.

No excessive bouncing, dramatic cinematic effects, or complicated interactions.

## 5. Technical Requirements

Preferred stack:
- React
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Lucide React

Requirements:
- Responsive, mobile-first design.
- Reusable components.
- Centralized editable content.
- Replaceable image assets.
- Smooth scrolling.
- Accessible interactive elements.
- No broken buttons or decorative fake interactions.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Keep the codebase lightweight and easy to deploy.

## 6. Creative Restrictions

This is a platonic birthday gift.

DO:
- Express genuine appreciation.
- Celebrate her individuality.
- Wish her well.
- Make the experience delightful.
- Keep the writing warm and natural.

DO NOT:
- Include romantic declarations.
- Use phrases like "my forever", "my favourite person", or "I can't imagine life without you".
- Suggest a shared romantic future.
- Include relationship references or emotional confessions.
- Make her feel obligated to respond emotionally.
- Turn the birthday website into a farewell letter.

## 7. Final Creative Direction

The website should feel like opening a small, beautifully wrapped digital gift.

Simple enough to explore in a minute or two, but thoughtful enough that she can tell someone actually put care into making it.

Preserve the elegant, whimsical aesthetic of the previous website, but keep this version lighter, more restrained, friendly, and genuinely celebratory.
